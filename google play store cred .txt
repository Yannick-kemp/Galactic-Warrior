## Why this fully fixes the Play rejection

The root cause was Unity falling back to the **debug keystore**. The preprocessor (an `IPreprocessBuildWithReport`) runs automatically before any Android build and forces `PlayerSettings.Android` to use your release keystore + passwords. Because Unity never saves passwords to disk, this is what guarantees the AAB is release-signed every session — and if `keystore.properties` is ever missing, the build **fails loudly** instead of silently producing a debug-signed file.
